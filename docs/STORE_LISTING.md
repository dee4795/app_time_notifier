# Google Play Store Listing — App Time Notifier

Use this text when filling out your Play Store listing in Play Console.

---

## App Name (max 30 characters)
`App Time Notifier`

## Short Description (max 80 characters)
`Gentle app time reminders with your own message and a loved one's photo.`

## Full Description
Spend less time scrolling without locking or blocking your apps.

**App Time Notifier** helps you stay mindful of how long you use distracting apps each day. Pick an app, set a daily time limit, write a personal message, and choose a photo of someone you love. When you reach your daily limit, you receive a gentle notification with your own message and photo.

**Why App Time Notifier?**
- **100% free:** Watch as many apps as you like with no limits, no subscriptions, and no ads.
- **No app blocking:** Your apps are never locked or blocked. You stay in control.
- **Personal & caring:** A reminder with your own words and a familiar photo feels much better than a strict timer.
- **100% private:** Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, and your photos and usage time never leave your device.

**Permissions explained:**
- **Usage access:** Used only on your phone to count how many minutes you used your chosen apps today.
- **Notifications:** Used to show your reminder when a daily limit is reached.
