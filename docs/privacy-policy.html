Privacy Policy — App Time Notifier

Last updated: November 2026

App Time Notifier is built with privacy first. Everything you set up in the app stays on your phone. We do not have a server, we do not create user accounts, and we do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data.

1. Information Used on Your Device

  • App Usage Time (Usage Access): If you turn on "Usage access" in Android Settings, the app checks how long you used your chosen apps today so it can send your reminder when the limit is reached. This usage data is read only on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • Photos & Reminder Messages: Any photo you choose and any message you write are copied into the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded or shared.
  • Installed App List: The app reads the list of launchable apps on your phone only so you can pick which apps to monitor.

2. Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Version 1 of App Time Notifier contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. If paid upgrades are offered through Google Play Billing, Google Play processes the payment according to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Deleting Your Data

Because all data is stored locally on your phone, removing an app from your list deletes its saved photo copy, and uninstalling App Time Notifier removes all app data from your device.
